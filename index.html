Building Worlds Since 2004

Founded in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel, Roblox started as a platform designed to let users create, explore, and share their own virtual worlds. Over the years, it has grown into a global online platform where millions of people can play games, express their creativity, build unique experiences, and connect with friends.
